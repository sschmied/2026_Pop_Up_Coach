How to film for the best results
  • Film from the side, at chest height, 3 to 5 metres away.
  • Keep the whole body in frame from head to toes, and the board.
  • Hold the camera steady in landscape, in good light, at 30 frames a second or more.
  • Film one surfer at a time if you can, and a few pop-ups in a row.

About the pro benchmark and the 10-point score
  • I could not view professional footage. The benchmark comes from published research on experienced surfers and from coaching accounts of the one-motion pop-up used on tour.
  • Speed: in-water time to pop-up was about 0.6 s in one study (Parsonage et al., 2020). A laboratory pop-up took 1.2 s overall, with knees near 100 degrees and elbows near 110 degrees at peak push (Sensors, 2021).
  • The ghost pops up in 0.6 s from the moment the surfer leaves the board, lands both feet together in a low crouch, and keeps its eyes up.
  • 10 means inside the benchmark range for speed, knee bend (95 to 125 degrees), foot width, torso lean, head position and steadiness. Those ranges are my coaching estimates, not published measurements of professionals.

Live Beach Challenge

Set a camera side-on. Lie flat, wait for the WAVE cue, then pop up. Coach times your reaction and pop-up, gives instant tips and records the whole challenge. On a laptop or desktop, the built-in or USB webcam works directly: stand a few metres back, side-on, so the camera sees your whole body. On a phone, open this page over HTTPS in Safari or Chrome.
